JOB DESCRIPTION Position: AI Engineer (Fresher / Entry-Level)
Experience: 0–1 year Employment Type: Full-time Location: [Company
Location] Department: Engineering / Artificial Intelligence

ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a curious and motivated AI Engineer to
join our team. This entry-level role is suitable for recent graduates
who have a strong foundation in Python, machine learning, and
problem-solving. You will work with experienced engineers to build,
test, and improve AI-powered applications and solutions.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Assist in developing, testing, and deploying
machine learning and AI-based applications. 2. Clean, preprocess,
analyze, and prepare structured and unstructured data for model
development. 3. Implement and evaluate machine learning algorithms using
Python libraries. 4. Explore and work with Generative AI concepts, Large
Language Models (LLMs), and prompt engineering. 5. Assist in building
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications and integrating LLM
APIs. 6. Work with frameworks and tools such as LangChain, LangGraph,
vector databases, and relevant AI platforms. 7. Develop and integrate
REST APIs for AI/ML services. 8. Evaluate model performance,
troubleshoot issues, and document experiments and results. 9.
Collaborate with software developers, data teams, and other stakeholders
to deliver AI features. 10. Stay updated with developments in artificial
intelligence and apply relevant techniques to projects.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS - Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science,
Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a
related field. - Fresh graduates and candidates with up to 1 year of
relevant experience may apply. - Good programming knowledge in Python. -
Understanding of machine learning concepts, including supervised and
unsupervised learning. - Familiarity with data handling and analysis
using NumPy and Pandas. - Basic understanding of statistics, data
preprocessing, and model evaluation. - Familiarity with SQL and
relational databases. - Understanding of APIs, JSON, and basic software
development practices. - Strong analytical, communication, and
problem-solving skills. - Willingness to learn, experiment, and work
collaboratively.

PREFERRED / GOOD-TO-HAVE SKILLS - Hands-on experience with Scikit-learn,
TensorFlow, or PyTorch. - Basic knowledge of deep learning, NLP, and
computer vision. - Exposure to Generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering,
and model APIs. - Familiarity with LangChain, LangGraph, or similar
orchestration frameworks. - Exposure to RAG pipelines, embeddings, and
vector databases such as FAISS, ChromaDB, or Pinecone. - Familiarity
with FastAPI or Flask. - Basic knowledge of Git, GitHub, Docker, and
cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud. - Academic,
internship, or personal projects demonstrating practical AI/ML skills.

WHAT WE OFFER - Opportunity to work on practical AI and machine learning
projects. - Mentorship and guidance from experienced team members. -
Exposure to modern AI tools, frameworks, and development practices. -
Opportunities to learn, contribute, and grow within the engineering
team.

HOW TO APPLY Interested candidates can submit their updated resume along
with links to relevant academic, internship, or personal projects.

Note: This is a sample job description for a fresher AI Engineer
position. The exact responsibilities and required skills may vary
depending on the company and project requirements.
